Hand over the project, not just the files.

Baton turns a departing member's project trail into a handover the next person can trust. It proves what the sources can prove, asks the person leaving only about what they can't, and shows each person only what they may see.

Start your own handover

The demo uses a synthetic student-club project: 91 made-up records with planted conflicts, gaps and secrets.

1. Connect

Exports any member can make: GitHub, WhatsApp, Trello, meeting notes.

2. Reconstruct

AI proposes small claims, each with a word-for-word quote.

3. Verify

Code checks every quote and finds conflicts, gaps and secrets.

4. Review

The leaver answers about 10 questions. A reviewer approves.

5. Hand over

The successor gets a role-scoped plan with a receipt on each line.
