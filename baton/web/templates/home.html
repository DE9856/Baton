Hand over the project, not just the files.

Baton proves what your project's records can prove, asks the person leaving only about what they can't, and shows each person only what they may see.

  1. Connect

  2. Reconstruct and verify

    AI proposes small claims. Code checks each quote and finds conflicts, gaps and secrets.

  3. Gap interview

    The person leaving answers about 10 questions, by tapping, typing or speaking.

  4. Review

    A reviewer resolves conflicts, decides the risky items and approves.

  5. Handover

    The next person gets a plan for their role, with a receipt behind each line.
