# Usability test script

The plan promises a test with 5 club members. **It has not been run.** This is the script. Write down what really happens, and never fill in results for someone who didn't take part. Use the synthetic demo only, so no consent for real data is needed.

## Setup

- One person at a time, about 25 minutes, on their own phone or laptop.
- Give them the link and say only: "This helps a club hand a project to the next person. Please try it and say what you think out loud."
- Don't explain anything unless they're stuck for 2 minutes. Note every time you help.

## Tasks

| # | Task to read out | Success means | Time target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Load the demo. You're Arjun, who is leaving. Answer the questions the app has for you." | Answers 9 questions | ≤ 20 min (plan §9.1) |
| 2 | "Now you're Sana, the president. Get the handover approved and shared." | Version 1 approved | ≤ 30 min |
| 3 | "Now you're Meera, taking over. What's the first thing due? Who owns the domain now? Is there a backup?" | All three right, using receipts | Note the time |
| 4 | "Ask the app something it can't know." | Understands "Not known" | Note |
| 5 | "Show me something Meera can't see, and why." | Uses "Viewing as" to compare | Note |
